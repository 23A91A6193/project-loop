2. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Modern digital companies collect feedback from fragmented channels: support tickets, App Store reviews, Google Play ratings, NPS surveys, sales calls, and community forums. Individually, these are isolated comments; collectively, they indicate urgent product fixes and strategic roadmap priorities.

Project LOOP solves this problem by providing a unified multi-tenant intelligence workspace:

  • Multi-Channel Ingestion: Single feedback forms, high-throughput bulk CSV uploads, and simulated real-time channel webhooks (Zendesk, App Store, G2).
  • AI Auto-Classification: Every incoming customer comment is analyzed in real time for sentiment, confidence scores, urgency level (L1–L5), and category tags.
  • Spike Detection: Compares week-over-week velocity to alert product teams before minor bugs become major churn events.
  • Ask LOOP (Grounded RAG): Natural language Q&A engine that answers product questions by citing verbatim customer comments with zero hallucinations.
  • VoC Executive Briefs: One-click generator producing leadership-ready Voice-of-Customer digests with exportable PDF formatting.

5. Key Features & Implementation Highlights

C1 & C2: Multi-Tenancy and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Project LOOP enforces strict workspace data segregation. Every single database query strictly filters by tenantId. The platform features three distinct pre-configured enterprise roles:

  • ADMIN: Full workspace governance, team member invites, API key management, and VoC report generation.
  • ANALYST: Feedback triage, CSV imports, manual AI re-classification, and status updates (NEW → REVIEWED → ACTIONED).
  • VIEWER: Read-only access to analytics and themes. Destructive or write actions trigger HTTP 403 Forbidden toast alerts.

C3 & C4: Feedback Ingestion & Dynamic Triage Workflow

Feedback can be ingested through single-entry forms, simulated webhooks, or CSV spreadsheets. The Inbox supports full-text search, status transitions, and multi-dimensional filtering across channel, sentiment, category, and date ranges with a dedicated one-click filter reset control.

AI1 & AI2: AI Auto-Classification & Trend Spike Detection

Customer submissions are automatically parsed into structured JSON containing sentiment scores, urgency ratings (1 to 5), and actionable business recommendations. Themes with greater than 40% week-over-week velocity growth are automatically flagged in a prominent red Spike Alert Banner.

AI3 & AI4: Ask LOOP (Grounded RAG) & VoC Executive Digests

The Ask LOOP assistant answers natural language questions (e.g., "What are users saying about mobile stability?") by fetching the top relevant customer records and generating a synthesis grounded strictly in verbatim quotes. Leadership teams can generate executive summaries and export them directly to PDF.
